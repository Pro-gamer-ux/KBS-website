01 / Installation

Plan a clear handover
from site to operation.

Coordinate access, site readiness, installation requirements and commissioning responsibilities before work starts.

↗

Installation planning

Review the work area, access, utilities and coordination needs for the equipment being installed.

◎

Commissioning & handover

Agree in advance on testing, documentation, training and acceptance steps in the project scope.

▤

Technical documentation

Specify the drawings, manuals, certificates and records required for your handover.

02 / Maintenance & repair

Keep equipment ready
for the work ahead.

Discuss preventive maintenance, repair and service planning around the operating duty and equipment condition.

⚙

Preventive maintenance

Build a maintenance conversation around equipment type, use, service history and operating environment.

↻

Repair & troubleshooting

Share the make, model, symptoms, photos and any fault codes so KBS can assess the next step.

◷

Planned service support

Ask about service scheduling and whether a maintenance agreement is suitable for your equipment.

03 / Inspection & assessment

Understand condition
before choosing a path.

A condition review can help frame repair, refurbishment or replacement discussions. Required inspection methods and formal reports should be agreed for each case.

◎

Condition assessment

Provide equipment details, age, usage, service history and known concerns for an initial review.

⌁

Refurbishment review

Explore repair, component renewal or modernisation options based on condition and compatibility.

▥

Inspection planning

Confirm applicable requirements, qualified inspection responsibilities, records and intervals for your location.

04 / Spare parts

Identify the part
and the equipment.

For parts enquiries, provide the equipment make, model, serial number, part number if known, and a photo of the component or nameplate.

Compatibility, availability, lead time and genuine-parts status should be confirmed before ordering.

05 / Upgrades & modernisation

Make existing systems
fit changing needs.

Discuss electrical controls, automation, safety-related improvements and equipment modernisation after reviewing the existing system and project requirements.

⌘

Controls & electrical upgrades

Review existing diagrams, panels, devices and interfaces before planning modifications.

↗

Automation integration

Describe the process, equipment and desired result for an integration discussion.

↻

Crane modernisation

Consider upgrade options alongside condition, safety, duty cycle and lifecycle needs.
