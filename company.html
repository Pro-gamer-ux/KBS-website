KBS Industrial Crane & Electromechanical Solutions Private Limited works with industrial and commercial customers on lifting, access and electromechanical requirements.

Who we are

A Kochi-based company focused on industrial movement and control.

From crane and lifting requirements to access control, parking and perimeter equipment, KBS helps customers explore practical system options for their sites.

The team is based in Kadavanthara, Kochi, and is available to discuss both defined projects and early-stage requirements.
