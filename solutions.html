04 / Crane types

Common industrial
crane categories.

A general reference for conversation. This list is not a statement of current inventory or guaranteed availability.

01 — MOBILE GANTRY

Mobile gantry crane

Freestanding crane configurations that can be moved as required by the system design.

02 — OVERHEAD

Overhead bridge crane

Building-supported lifting systems for defined work areas and travel paths.

03 — LOCAL LIFT

Jib crane

Localised lifting around a workstation or service area.

04 — GANTRY

Gantry & semi-gantry

Freestanding or partially supported crane arrangements.

05 — SPECIALIST

Process & steel mill cranes

Specialist industrial categories requiring careful engineering review.

06 — OTHER TYPES

RTG, mobile & monorail

Additional common categories to consider based on site and operation.

Actual capacity, dimensions, compliance and suitability must be confirmed by qualified project review.
