KBS INDUSTRIAL WEBSITE

Open index.html to view the homepage. Keep all files together:
- index.html, solutions.html, industries.html, support.html, catalogues.html, explorer.html, company.html, contact.html, knowledge.html, planner.html
- site.css, kb.css, site.js, carousel.js, knowledge.js and explorer.js
- assets/kbs-logo.png, the supplied crane photos and film, and lightweight SVG illustrations

The site is static. KBS Quick Answers and the searchable FAQs use knowledge.js and make no AI API calls. The assistant is an offline information guide, not a staffed live chat. Long answers open under “More detail.” The planner prepares a message for WhatsApp or email; it does not size equipment. The contact form opens the visitor’s email app and does not store enquiries on a server.

The home carousel uses the supplied crane photographs and video. Photos rotate automatically; visitors can pause or change slides. The short video loads only when its slide is selected and must be played by the visitor. Explore KBS Solutions keeps the choice simple: choose a category, then choose Buy, Rent or Refurbish for cranes.

Products & Services covers crane sales, rental, refurbishment and modernisation, lifting systems, parking, access control, perimeter security, and electrical/automation enquiries. Service & Support covers installation, maintenance, repair, inspection planning, spare parts and upgrades. Equipment availability, technical ratings and exact project scope are confirmed for each job.

Catalogues has category links ready. When official company PDFs are provided, add them to assets/catalogues and link the right files from catalogues.html.

To publish free with GitHub Pages:
1. Upload all site files and the assets folder to the repository root, preserving folder structure.
2. In repository Settings > Pages, publish the main branch from /(root).
3. Keep your custom domain in Pages settings.
4. At GoDaddy, keep the GitHub Pages A records and www CNAME. Keep MX and TXT records used for email.
5. After DNS verification and TLS setup, enable Enforce HTTPS.

KBS is based in Kadavanthara, Kochi, Kerala. Update company-specific wording when service details change. The interactive explorer and planner help organise enquiries; they do not make engineering decisions.
